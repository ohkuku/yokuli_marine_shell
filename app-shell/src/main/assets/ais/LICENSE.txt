Yokuli AIS procedural traffic geometry, 2026.

The accompanying generate_models.py and traffic-*.glb models are original
procedural work released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No third-party meshes, textures, terrain or nautical chart data are included.
Ship appearance and vertical dimensions are symbolic, not measured identity.
Horizontal dimensions and antenna offsets are applied only when received and
usable; unknown heading uses an orientation-neutral geometry.
